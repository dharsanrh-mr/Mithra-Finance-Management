Mithra Finance System Finance App V5 - Collections V2
Features:
- Today's / Week / Month collection summaries
- Outstanding and overdue tracking
- Today's due collection cards
- Overdue follow-up picker
- Search by customer, mobile, receipt or loan
- Date and payment-mode filters
- Cash / UPI / Bank totals
- Quick payment amounts
- Payment reference / UTR and notes
- Automatic loan/customer balance sync
- Receipt generation with print/PDF and WhatsApp
- LocalStorage persistence
